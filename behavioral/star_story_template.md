# STAR+Data Story Template

Use one real story from your own experience. Do not memorize a fictional answer.

## Situation

- What business/data problem existed?
- Who depended on the data?
- What was at risk?

## Task

- What outcome were you personally responsible for?
- What constraints made it difficult?

## Action

Explain your decisions, not just team activity.

- Investigation / root cause
- Technical design or fix
- Tradeoff you made
- Stakeholder communication
- Validation mechanism
- Operational mechanism added afterward

## Result

Use measurable evidence where possible:

- freshness / latency
- failure rate
- data-quality incidents
- query/runtime reduction
- cloud or warehouse cost
- manual hours saved
- adoption or business impact

## Reflection

- What did you learn?
- What would you do differently?
- What permanent mechanism prevented recurrence?

## Reuse map

A strong data-engineering story can often support themes such as ownership, diving deep, high standards, simplification, customer impact, delivering results, and constructive disagreement.
